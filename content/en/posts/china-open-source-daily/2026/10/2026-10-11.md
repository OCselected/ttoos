### ⚖️ Legal & Licensing — SASAC Decree 46 on Lifetime Reckoning for Pseudo-Domestic Xinchuang Procurement Plus the June 2026 Full-Stack-Xinchuang-Audit New Rules Plus State Council Office Document 2025-34 Domestic-Product Bidding Preference: Three Policy Lines Tighten Into One Accountability Surface — With the Cobra Effect Naming the Forgery-Technology-Amplification Mechanism Explicitly

**2. Domestic database outlet (via 国产数据库 official channel, 2026-10-10) — "Purchasing pseudo-domestic is now lifetime-reckoning; Xinchuang selection criteria have changed again; the cobra effect is only intensifying"**

**3. State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission of the State Council — Measures for Investigating and Holding Accountable for Violations in Central Enterprise Operations and Investment (SASAC Decree No. 46)**

**4. Ministry of Industry and Information Technology + Central Cyberspace Affairs Commission + Central Government Procurement Center (June 2026) — New rules on government, party, and state-informatization new-and-modified systems requiring full-stack Xinchuang adaptation and code-chain-full-link audit**

**5. State Council Office Document No. 2025-34 — Government procurement domestic-product standard; domestic products enjoy bidding price-review preference from this year**

**6. SASAC Instruction No. 79 — Xinchuang transformation completion target: end of 2027, full replacement, "should replace, must replace"**

**7. First-quarter 2026 financial Xinchuang tender data — 40 percent+ year-on-year growth; full-year Xinchuang market projection 2.66 trillion yuan**

**8. National Testing Announcement No. 1 of 2023 — 11 database evaluation certifications expiring 2026-12-25; non-renewal invalidates eligibility**

The 2026-10-10 domestic-database-outlet piece on SASAC Decree No. 46 is the first-documented instance in this series of the Xinchuang-institutional-surface being operationalized at the lifetime-reckoning-and-personalized-reckoning layer simultaneously with the cobra-effect-plus-forgery-technology-amplification layer. The piece reads the three policy lines together:

**The SASAC Decree 46 line.** Decree No. 46 is titled "Measures for Investigating and Holding Accountable for Violations in Central Enterprise Operations and Investment." Article 12 writes "producing, selling, or purchasing pseudo-innovation and pseudo-domestic products" into the accountability list. The core message: buying a relabeled or shoddy fake-domestic product is no longer just a "project-failure" question — it is a lifetime accountability question for the person who signed off. Article 4 sets **lifetime accountability for major decisions**. Article 35 specifies that **collective decision members are held separately** — collective decision is not an exemption. Article 51 closes off the escape route: **retirement, benefit adjustment, transfer, resignation — accountability continues**, and where state-asset loss is caused, compensation obligations can be pursued. Penalties are maximum severity: direct responsible persons face 100 percent deduction of annual performance pay plus 100 percent deduction of term incentives plus forfeiture of the preceding three years' medium- and long-term incentive income, with severe cases leading to demotion and lifetime prohibition from state-owned enterprise service.

**The June 2026 MIIT-CAC-Central-Purchasing-Procurement-Center line.** New rules require that government, party, and state-informatization new-and-modified systems meet **full-stack Xinchuang adaptation** and **code-chain-full-link audit** requirements. Systems that do not meet the bar are disqualified from bidding and acceptance. This is a compliance gate on the procurement-award side.

**The State Council Office Document No. 2025-34 line.** From this year, government procurement establishes a **domestic-product standard** and **domestic products enjoy bidding price-review preference**. This is a compliance gate on the market-entry side.

**The SASAC Instruction No. 79 timeline pressure.** Instruction No. 79's target is **end of 2027 completion of Xinchuang transformation, full replacement, "should replace, must replace."** Only slightly more than a year remains from the 2026-10-10 publication date.

**The market-scale backdrop.** Q1 2026 financial Xinchuang tender scale rose more than 40 percent year-on-year; the full-year 2026 Xinxuang market is projected at **2.66 trillion yuan**. Volume is expanding rapidly.

**The National Testing Announcement No. 1 of 2023 expiration.** 11 database products entered the national testing registry under 2023 Announcement No. 1 — the **evaluation validity period expires 2026-12-25**. Products not re-certified before then lose eligibility. This is a compliance gate on the vendor-product-eligibility side.

**Institutional significance: The 2026-10-10 SASAC Decree 46 piece is the first-documented instance in this series of the Xinchuang-southeast-side-of-stratification institutional surface being operationalized at the lifetime-reckoning-and-personalized-reckoning layer simultaneously with the cobra-effect-plus-forgery-technology-amplification layer — a two-surface-simultaneous institutional-form observation paired with this series' Oct 9 briefing's documentation of the Xinchuang-northwest-side-of-stratification (head-of-stratification slowdown) institutional surface being operationalized at the value-based-rollout-and-business-first-and-risk-controlled layer.**

From an institutional economics perspective, the Decree 46 piece matters on four axes:

**First, the three-line pairing (SASAC Decree 46 lifetime accountability + June 2026 full-stack-audit new rules + State Council Office Document 2025-34 domestic-product bidding preference) is institutionally the conversion of Xinchuang procurement from "support domestic" to "personally accountable" — a structurally new institutional-economics observation on the same institutional object this series' Oct 9 briefing documented at the head-of-stratification slowdown layer.** The Decree 46 piece's sharpest institutional sentence is: "The three lines are twisted together — the direction is clear: Xinchuang procurement is shifting from supporting domestic products to personal accountability." From an institutional economics standpoint, this **support-domestic-narrative / personal-accountability-narrative pairing** is a **structural finding**: Xinchuang procurement has been documented in this series at the administrative-narrative-support layer (this series' Aug 3, Aug 19, Sep 11, Sep 18, Sep 23, Sep 30, Oct 2, Oct 4, Oct 8 briefings) and at the value-based-rollout-and-business-first layer (this series' Oct 9 briefing), but not at the personal-accountability layer. The institutional-economics reading is that Xinchuang procurement is now a three-layer institutional object: (i) administrative-narrative-support layer, (ii) value-based-rollout-and-business-first layer, (iii) personal-accountability-and-lifetime-reckoning layer. The three layers are not alternatives — they are simultaneously operating surfaces on the same institutional object, and Decree 46 is the third layer being activated.

**Second, the individual-victim-selection-logic inversion (from "cheapest and pretty-parameter" to "won't get in trouble") is an institutional-economics fact about the cobra-effect-plus-evidence-chain-hard-currency transition.** The Decree 46 piece explicitly names the selection-logic inversion: "In the past, one chose the cheapest, the pretty-parameter. Now one chooses the non-incident-risk." And: "How the tender document is written is how the evidence chain must be preserved." Source code, audit reports, national-testing evaluation level, real deployment cases — the items that used to be bonus items are now becoming hard currency. From an institutional economics standpoint, this **cheap-and-pretty-selection / non-incident-risk-selection pairing** is a **structural finding**: Xinchuang procurement has been converted from a quality-and-cost-optimization problem (cheapest + pretty-parameter) into a risk-and-accountability-avoidance problem (non-incident-risk + full evidence chain). The institutional-economics reading is that the accountability layer is not just adding a compliance gate — it is **inverting the selection-optimization objective function** from quality-cost trade-off to risk-avoidance-first. This is a structurally new institutional-form observation about procurement as an optimization problem whose objective function has been rewritten.

**Third, the explicit cobra-effect-plus-Yangquan-horse-skin-analogy pairing is an institutional-economics fact about the forgery-technology-amplification mechanism.** The Decree 46 piece explicitly invokes the cobra-effect — the classic case in which colonial India offered a bounty per dead cobra to reduce cobra populations, resulting in people breeding cobras to kill them for the bounty, and after the bounty was cancelled releasing the bred cobras into the wild, increasing the cobra population. It also invokes the 1980s Yangquan (Shanxi) horse-skin bounty case — where people killed healthy horses to sell their hide for the bounty, increasing horse deaths. The piece's sharpest institutional sentence: "All three Xinchuang policy lines are triggering the cobra effect — Decree 46's lifetime-reckoning for pseudo-domestic will cause vendors to research how to forge real domestic; full-stack Xinchuang plus code-chain audit will cause vendors to research how to forge auditable evidence chains; State Council Office Document 2025-34's domestic-product standard will cause vendors to research how to forge domestic-product status." From an institutional economics standpoint, this **cobra-effect-narrative / forgery-technology-amplification-mechanism pairing** is a **structural finding**: the accountability-and-compliance-layer addition is not a one-way ratchet — it is a two-way incentive structure in which the stricter the compliance and the higher the personal accountability, the higher the forgery-technology incentive. This is not a critique of Decree 46 or the compliance layer — it is an empirical observation that compliance-and-accountability layers, in isolation, activate forgery-technology amplification, and that the only institutional answer to the forgery-technology-amplification mechanism is code-level proof-of-domestic (the Decree 46 piece's sharpest closing sentence: "Whoever can prove 'I am real domestic' to the code level can make it into the next short list"). The institutional-economics reading is that the accountability layer has activated a forgery-technology counter-selection — a **selection pressure on the vendor side** that the accountability layer itself does not directly address.

**Fourth, the "more orders but fewer signers" paradox (2.66 trillion yuan annual Xinchuang market + Q1 financial Xinchuang 40%+ year-on-year tender growth + 79 Instruction 2027 completion target + Decree 46 lifetime accountability) is an institutional-economics fact about the compliance-amplified market-volume-amplified accountability-amplified signing-contracted paradox.** From an institutional economics standpoint, this **market-volume-amplification / signing-contracting pairing** is a **structural finding**: Xinchuang is now a market in which volume is expanding (2.66 trillion yuan annual, 40%+ Q1 financial growth), accountability is expanding (Decree 46 lifetime-reckoning), timeline is contracting (79 Instruction 2027 completion), and vendor-eligibility is contracting (2023 Announcement No. 1 expiration 2026-12-25). All four institutional pressures are moving in the direction of a smaller signing population in a larger market. The institutional-economics reading — this piece's sharpest institutional sentence — is that the volume-amplification and accountability-amplification pairing is not producing a "healthy market" (in the sense of more participants and more accountability) — it is producing a **compressed signing population in an expanding market** — a structurally new institutional-form observation.

The two stories together document that **two Chinese-institutional surfaces this series' prior Oct briefings have documented at the administrative-construction-adjacent layer are now being operationalized at two new institutional layers simultaneously**:

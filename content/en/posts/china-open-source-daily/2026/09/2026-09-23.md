## China Open Source Daily — 2026-09-23

### Institutional Change — Xiamen Trustworthy Agent Interconnection Center Launches Trial Run Under the MIIT 15th-Five-Year-Plan Internet Infrastructure Layer

**1. Xiamen Trustworthy Agent Interconnection Center (September 18, 2026) — HelloAgent series launch · CAICT + China Internet Society + three major telecom carriers + Fujian Broadband + Xiamen municipal government co-hosting · MIIT four-ministry July guidance + MIIT 15th-Five-Year-Plan (August) · Agent + DID + Blockchain fusion · Internet Gateway as next-generation Internet infrastructure**

On September 18, 2026, the Xiamen Trustworthy Agent Interconnection Center (智能体标识与身份网络暨智能体可信互联中心 厦门) held its trial-run launch meeting — the second-stop launch of the HelloAgent series of release activities, marking the transition from a Beijing single-point validation to a national integrated-network deployment pattern.

**The joint-hosting apparatus.** Per the HelloAgent organizing report, the center is co-hosted by the China Academy of Information and Communications Technology (CAICT / 中国信通院), the China Internet Society (CIS / 中国互联网协会), the three major telecom carriers (China Mobile / China Unicom / China Telecom), Fujian Broadband Network (福建广电), and the Xiamen municipal government. The launch meeting was framed as the second-stop in the HelloAgent series of release activities, with the organizing report explicitly pairing the Xiamen trial run with a prior Beijing single-point validation and positioning the next step as "national integrated-layout phased construction and interconnection validation."

**The regulatory basis.** The launch is documented as being operationalized under two specific national-regulatory instruments:

- **July 2026, MIIT and three other ministries** — the joint "Guiding Opinions on Promoting High-Quality Development of Internet Basic Resources" (《关于推动互联网基础资源高质量发展的指导意见》), which per the HelloAgent report "explicitly requires exploring the construction of an interconnection network infrastructure for intelligent agents with a unified communication protocol, data interaction standard, and interface specification."
- **August 2026, MIIT** — the "Information and Communications Industry 15th-Five-Year-Plan" (《信息通信行业发展"十五五"规划》), which per the HelloAgent report "further proposes accelerating the development of an intelligent agent interconnection network, building a new-generation Internet infrastructure of intelligent network integration, constructing an agent network identity system, upgrading the Internet Domain Name System, establishing an Internet agent identity identification system, strengthening the application of blockchain and electronic certification in trustworthy identity authentication, and building an Internet Gateway to construct foundational capabilities for agent registration management, identity authentication, ownership determination, addressing, and resolution."

The 15th-Five-Year-Plan document is the **first-documented instance in this series of the MIIT's 15th-Five-Year-Plan for the Information and Communications Industry being operationalized at the municipal-trial-run-center layer simultaneously with the Beijing single-point-validation layer and the national-integrated-network-deployment layer**. This pairing places the CAICT administrative-institute surface at three institutionally distinct layers simultaneously — the CAICT-administrative-institute standardization-instrument layer (this briefing), the CAICT-administrative-institute third-party-assessment-and-testing-system layer (AOMA, September 20 briefing), and the MIIT/CESI industry-standard layer (SJ/T 12304-2026, September 19 briefing) — and constitutes a first-documented MIIT-15th-Five-Year-Plan-plus-three-ministry-guidance-plus-CAICT-plus-CIS-plus-three-major-carriers-plus-municipal-government co-hosting surface on the Chinese national-standardization apparatus.

**The fusion technology route.** The organizing report of HelloAgent's launch explicitly pairs the national-standardization apparatus with a technical architecture: "adopting an 'agent + DID (Decentralized Identifier) + blockchain' fusion technology route." The HelloAgent report further documents that the Xiamen center is "based on the Xinhuo Chain Super Node (Xiamen) foundation, fully leveraging the domestic and international dual-node advantages, basing itself in Xiamen and radiating toward BRICS nations."

The **agent + DID + blockchain fusion technology route** is a **structurally new institutional-form observation** at the Chinese national-standardization apparatus. This route pairs the DID (Decentralized Identifier) layer — an international W3C-standardized identifier technology — with the blockchain layer — a domestic-Xinhuo-Chain-operationalized infrastructure — and with the agent-layer — an AI-agent-identity standard being operationalized at the municipal-trial-run-center layer. The pairing is institutionally distinctive: it does not merely place the agent layer under a centralized administrative registry but constructs a decentralized-identifier-plus-blockchain-infrastructure-plus-agent-identity triangle.

**The Internet Gateway as next-generation Internet infrastructure.** The HelloAgent report documents that the Internet Gateway (互联网关) is being positioned as the "next-generation Internet infrastructure," with foundational capabilities explicitly including "agent registration management, identity authentication, ownership determination, addressing, and resolution." The organizing report describes the Xiamen deployment as moving "from Beijing to Xiamen, from single-point validation to a national integrated-layout," and positions the Xiamen center as a stepping stone for the phased construction of additional agent trustworthy interconnection centers nationwide.

**The open-source-community cross-domain participation layer.** The HelloAgent report documents that the ANP (Agent Network Protocol) open-source community founder Chang Gaowei (常高伟) and the CAICT Industrial Internet and IoT Research Institute International Department business-development lead Yu Xiaoyu (于潇豫) jointly interpreted the "trade intelligent agent Internet open collaboration pathway." This is the **first-documented instance in this series of an independent open-source community (ANP) participating in the same institutional forum as a MIIT-affiliated research institute at the national-standardization apparatus layer** — a structurally new institutional-form object at the Chinese national-standardization apparatus. The ANP community is positioned as an "open-source community," while the CAICT Industrial Internet and IoT Research Institute is positioned as a "MIIT-affiliated research institute." The two actors share the same institutional forum at the same national-standardization apparatus layer.

**The "Hundred Scenes Thousand Intelligences" multi-agent collaboration initiative.** The HelloAgent report documents that the CAICT Industrial Internet and IoT Research Institute's Senior Engineer Fan Sen introduced the "Hundred Scenes Thousand Intelligences Multi-Agent Collaboration Scenario Innovation Action Plan" (百景千智多智能体协同场景创新行动计划). This is the **first-documented instance in this series of a CAICT-administrative-institute-scenario-innovation-action-plan layer being operationalized at the municipal-trial-run-center-plus-national-standardization-apparatus layer simultaneously**.

**Institutional significance:** The Xiamen Trustworthy Agent Interconnection Center's trial-run launch is the **first-documented instance of the MIIT 15th-Five-Year-Plan for the Information and Communications Industry being operationalized at the municipal-trial-run-center layer simultaneously with the Beijing single-point-validation layer and the national-integrated-network-deployment layer**, and it is being operationalized at the agent-identity-and-identity-network layer with an 'agent + DID + blockchain' fusion technology route, an Internet Gateway as the next-generation Internet infrastructure, and an ANP open-source community participating at the same institutional forum as a MIIT-affiliated research institute — a first-documented MIIT-15th-Five-Year-Plan-plus-three-ministry-guidance-plus-CAICT-plus-CIS-plus-three-major-carriers-plus-municipal-government-plus-open-source-community co-hosting surface on the Chinese national-standardization apparatus.

### Institutional Change — Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 Open-Weight License and the First-Documented Open-Weight Frontier-Model Commercial-Monetization Thresholds

**2. Moonshot AI / Kimi K3 (September 11–21, 2026) — Kimi K3 open-weight license with US$20M revenue threshold and 100M MAU attribution · Moonshot AI US$2B annualized revenue target for 2026 year-end · US$50B valuation with Goldman Sachs / CICC / Deutsche Bank HK IPO underwriting · Microsoft / Amazon / Google cloud-hosting negotiations at up to 30% revenue-share · 300 billion tokens per day on OpenRouter**

Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 open-weight model is the **first-documented case of a Chinese frontier-AI-lab open-weight model license placing a specific commercial-monetization threshold on Model-as-a-Service deployment**, and it is being operationalized simultaneously with (a) a confidential Hong Kong IPO filing at a US$50 billion valuation with three named global underwriting advisors, (b) a US$2 billion annualized-revenue target for 2026 year-end (up from US$1 billion in August, US$300 million in June, US$200 million in April), and (c) active negotiations with Microsoft, Amazon, and Google for up to a 30% revenue-share hosting arrangement on their cloud platforms.

**The Kimi K3 open-weight license's commercial-monetization threshold.** Per Tech Wire Asia's summary of Moonshot's Kimi K3 license terms, "a company operating a Model-as-a-Service business must enter into a separate agreement with Moonshot before using K3 commercially if the company and its affiliates generate more than US$20 million in aggregate revenue over any consecutive 12-month period." The license defines Model-as-a-Service as "providing third parties with access to model inference or fine-tuning through services such as an API." The license further places an attribution requirement "on large commercial products using K3 ... if they exceed 100 million monthly active users or US$20 million in monthly revenue."

The **US$20M aggregate-revenue threshold / Model-as-a-Service deployment / large-commercial-product attribution requirement pairing** is a **structurally new institutional-form observation** on the Chinese frontier-AI open-weight commercial-monetization layer. The license is not a permissive FLOSS-style open-weight license but a **hybrid open-weight license** that preserves open-weight distribution and modification rights while reserving the right to require a separate commercial agreement above a specific revenue threshold. From a Williamson L1→L4 institutional-economics reading, this is a **governance-mechanism-level intervention** at the L3 layer that preserves the L4 resource-allocation openness of open-weight distribution while constraining the commercial-monetization pathway. The license does not convert open-weight into closed-weight; it converts open-weight commercial-monetization above a specific revenue threshold into a contractually governed pathway.

**The US$2B annualized revenue target and the margin gap with closed-weight peers.** Per Bloomberg's reporting as summarized by Tech Wire Asia, Moonshot told investors that annual recurring revenue exceeded US$1 billion in August, up from US$300 million in June, and had been "generating more than US$200 million in annual recurring revenue in April." The US$2B figure represents a year-end annualized revenue target rather than revenue booked across the full year. Per TechCrunch's September 11 reporting, "Moonshot's projected revenue is still dwarfed by that of OpenAI and Anthropic, which recent reports put at $40 billion and $65 billion, respectively. Because Moonshot's model weights are freely available, the company has far lower margins than its closed-weight competitors. The rising projections show there's still money to be made from open-weight AI models, even if they're not as lucrative as closed-weight frontier models."

The **open-weight-vs-closed-weight-margin-asymmetry** documented by TechCrunch is the sharpest institutional-economics object of the story: at the L4 resource-allocation layer, the open-weight Kimi K3 model generates US$1B ARR (August 2026) with far lower margins than the closed-weight OpenAI (US$40B) and Anthropic (US$65B) revenue streams. The institutional-economics reading is that the open-weight Kimi K3 is operating at a **structurally lower margin band** than the closed-weight frontier peers, with the revenue growth trajectory (US$200M in April → US$300M in June → US$1B in August → US$2B target year-end) documenting a specific open-weight-monetization growth curve that is distinct from the closed-weight frontier-monetization curve. This is a first-documented instance in this series of an open-weight-frontier-model margin curve being documented at the same reporting layer as a closed-weight-frontier-model margin curve.

**The US$50B HK IPO underwriting apparatus.** Per Reuters' reporting as summarized by Tech Wire Asia, Moonshot has "confidentially filed for a Hong Kong initial public offering that could seek about US$3 billion," with "Goldman Sachs, China International Capital Corp, and Deutsche Bank" working as underwriting advisors. The reporting further documents that "the company was valued at about US$50 billion in an ongoing funding round at the time of the filing." Prior funding was documented as "about US$2 billion in May at a valuation above US$20 billion, followed by a US$3.5 billion round in July that valued Moonshot at US$35 billion," with the July round exceeding its initial target of "between US$1 billion and US$2 billion." Cumulative funding since founding is documented as "more than US$5.5 billion."

The **US$50B valuation-plus-three-global-underwriting-advisors** pairing is institutionally parallel to this series' September 12 briefing's documentation of Moonshot AI's SCMP-documented dual-listing exploration (Hong Kong + Shanghai STAR Market) and this series' September 1 briefing's documentation of the National AI Industry Investment Fund's lead-investor role at Moonshot's $35B valuation. The Moonshot surface is being operationalized at three institutionally distinct capital-market layers simultaneously: the state-directed-capital lead-investor layer (National AI Industry Investment Fund), the confidential-HK-IPO-filing layer (Goldman Sachs / CICC / Deutsche Bank), and the SCMP-documented dual-listing-exploration layer (STAR Market + HK). This is a **three-surface-simultaneous Chinese-frontier-AI-capital-market-layer operationalization pattern**.

**The Microsoft / Amazon / Google cloud-hosting negotiations at 30% revenue-share.** Per Reuters' August 2026 reporting as summarized by Tech Wire Asia, "Moonshot was in early-stage negotiations with Microsoft, Amazon, and Google to host K3 on their cloud platforms, with the company seeking as much as a 30% share of revenue generated from K3-related services." The reporting further documents that "the talks also covered data access and mechanisms for auditing token usage," and that "Moonshot had already reached similar revenue-sharing arrangements with smaller cloud providers, including Chinasoft International." The negotiations remained active into September.

The **up-to-30-percent-revenue-share cloud-hosting arrangement with Microsoft / Amazon / Google** is a **structurally new institutional-form observation** at the Chinese frontier-AI open-weight distribution layer. The Kimi K3 open-weight model — which is technically distributed as open weights subject to Moonshot's license conditions — is being operationalized at a **foreign-cloud-platform-hosted-inference-with-revenue-share layer** simultaneously with a **Chinese-cloud-platform-hosted-inference-with-revenue-share layer** (Chinasoft International) and a **Moonshot's own service-hosted-inference layer**. From an institutional economics standpoint, the Kimi K3 open-weight distribution is being operationalized at three institutionally distinct distribution channels simultaneously — the Chinese-cloud layer, the foreign-cloud layer, and the vendor's own service layer — with the 30% revenue-share apparatus functioning as a specific governance-mechanism-level intervention at the L3 layer.

**The open-weight-license-vs-model-distillation-accusations tension.** Per TechCrunch's September 11 reporting, "Anthropic accused the company [Moonshot AI] of a long-running model distillation campaign that routed nearly 300,000 requests from Kimi directly to Claude Opus, effectively serving Opus in place of Kimi's own models. In total, more than 23 million responses were collected from Anthropic models for use in Moonshot's training." Per the Anthropic Threat Intelligence Report of September 2026, this constitutes a specific model-distillation accusation against Moonshot AI.

The **open-weight-distribution-plus-model-distillation-accusation** pairing is the sharpest institutional-economics object of the story: Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 open-weight model is being operationalized at the open-weight-distribution layer simultaneously with an active model-distillation accusation from Anthropic at the model-ip-and-training-data-sourcing layer. From an institutional economics standpoint, the same Moonshot entity is being documented at two institutionally distinct institutional-form layers simultaneously — the open-weight-distribution layer (Kimi K3 open-weight license with commercial-monetization thresholds) and the model-ip-and-training-data-sourcing layer (Anthropic's model-distillation accusation). This is a **first-documented instance in this series of a Chinese frontier-AI-lab open-weight model being operationalized at the open-weight-distribution layer simultaneously with an active model-distillation accusation from a Western frontier-AI-lab**.

**Institutional significance:** Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 open-weight model is the **first-documented instance of a Chinese frontier-AI-lab open-weight model license placing a specific commercial-monetization threshold (US$20M aggregate revenue, 100M MAU attribution) on Model-as-a-Service deployment in this series**, and it is being operationalized at the Moonshot-AI-Kimi-K3 open-weight-license-plus-commercial-monetization-threshold layer simultaneously with (a) a US$50B valuation confidential-HK-IPO-filing-plus-three-global-underwriting-advisors layer, (b) a US$2B annualized-revenue-target-plus-open-weight-vs-closed-weight-margin-gap layer, (c) a Microsoft-Amazon-Google-cloud-hosting-plus-30-percent-revenue-share layer, and (d) an active Anthropic model-distillation accusation layer — a four-surface-simultaneous Chinese-frontier-AI-open-weight-commercial-monetization pattern.

### Institutional Change — Tiangong Kaiwu Foundation's 11-Real-Task List: From Ecosystem Co-Builder Day to Foundational Instructional-Curator Layer

**3. Tiangong Kaiwu Open Source Foundation (September 20–22, 2026) — First batch of 11 concrete ecosystem-operations tasks · KHub rare-disease open source community task · Datawhale co-hosting · Tiangong Kaiwu Deputy Secretary-General Zhuang Biao-wei facilitation · Closing assignment submission deadline at noon September 22 · Demo Day on September 23**

The Tiangong Kaiwu Open Source Foundation's AI Open-Source Ecosystem Operations Class — the class this series' September 20 briefing documented at the Chinese municipal-district-party-committee-talent-office-plus-university-plus-open-source-foundation-plus-education-platform-plus-weekly-event co-hosting layer during the GOAI Open Source Week Ecosystem Co-Builder Day — published its first batch of 11 concrete ecosystem-operations tasks on September 20, 2026. The class's closing assignment submission deadline was at 12:00 noon on September 22, 2026, with approximately 20 participants advancing to the offline Demo Day on September 23.

**The 11-task list.** Per the Tiangong Kaiwu Foundation's Datawhale-hosted organizing report, the first batch of 11 tasks is:

1. Let university events settle into sustained communities (让高校活动沉淀为持续社群)
2. Push rare-disease needs to form a co-building project — KHub Rare-Disease Open Source Community (推动罕见病需求形成共建项目 KHub 罕见病开源社区)
3. Recruit AI industry-operations co-building partners (招募 AI 产业运营共建伙伴)
4. Run a real café with AI (用 AI 经营一家真实咖啡馆)
5. Produce a Zhuzhou-city talent-promotion video (制作株洲市人才宣传片)
6. Produce an AI-plus-industrial case study topic video (制作 AI+工业典型案例专题片)
7. Design an AI implementation industrial training program (设计 AI 落地产业培训)
8. Co-build a cute-pet short-video account (共建萌宠短视频账号)
9. Participate in DELTA SPACE space operations (参与 DELTA SPACE 空间运营)
10. Turn free GPU experience into real adoption — AMD ROCm (让免费 GPU 体验走向真实采用 AMD ROCm)

**The methodological formula.** The organizing report explicitly pairs the 11-task list with a five-step operational formula: "find real needs → find the right people → design the participation path → enable the first action → let contributions keep happening" (找到真实需求 → 找到合适的人 → 设计参与路径 → 促成第一次行动 → 让贡献持续发生).

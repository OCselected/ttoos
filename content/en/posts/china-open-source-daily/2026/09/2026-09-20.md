## China Open Source Daily — 2026-09-20

### Institutional Change — Tiangong Kaiwu Open Source Foundation Launches China's First AI Open-Source Ecosystem Operation Class, Co-Hosted with a District-Committee Talent Office

**1. Tiangong Kaiwu Open Source Foundation WeChat (2026-09-19) — AI Open-Source Ecosystem Operation Class · Co-Builder Day Recruitment · 首届生态共建者日招募**

The Tiangong Kaiwu Open Source Foundation (天工开物开源基金会), the Beijing-based open-source foundation this series' August 8 briefing documented at the CAICT-co-founded OAAIF layer and this series' September 2 briefing documented at the Chongqing 15th-Five-Year-Plan AI-framework-analysis layer, published on September 19, 2026 the recruitment announcement for the inaugural **AI Open-Source Ecosystem Operation Class (人工智能开源生态运营班) and Ecosystem Co-Builder Day (生态共建者日)** — the first-documented Chinese-municipal-district-party-committee-talent-office-plus-university-plus-open-source-foundation-plus-education-platform plus weekly-event co-hosting surface in this series.

The announcement's sharpest institutional-economics fact is its **co-host structure**: **Tiangong Kaiwu Open Source Foundation + Xihu District Committee Talent Office (西湖区委组织部人才办) + Hangzhou Dianzi University (杭州电子科技大学) + Datawhale**. This is a four-party co-hosting arrangement in which a Chinese-municipal-district-party-committee-talent-office is placed as a co-host alongside an open-source foundation, a public university, and an open-source education platform — the first-documented instance of a Chinese-municipal-district-party-committee-talent-office appearing as a formal co-host on the same institutional surface as a Chinese open-source foundation, a Chinese public university, and a Chinese open-source education platform. From an institutional economics standpoint, this **district-party-committee-talent-office-plus-open-source-foundation-plus-university-plus-education-platform plus weekly-event pairing** is a **structural finding**: the same Chinese open-source foundation surface this series' prior thirty-one briefings have documented at multiple layers (the OAAIF co-founding layer, the Chongqing-plan-analysis layer, the CANN HCCL innovation-contest-scale layer) is being operationalized at the Chinese-municipal-district-party-committee-talent-office co-hosting layer simultaneously — a structurally new institutional-form observation.

The announcement's operational profile, verbatim from the Tiangong Kaiwu text:
- **Dates:** The Ecosystem Co-Builder Day (首届生态共建者日) will be held on September 20, 2026 during the **GOAI Open Source Week 2026**
- **Launch event reference:** The program held its launch ceremony at Hangzhou West Lake in May 2026
- **Program structure:** A pre-screening + demand-matching mechanism in which project owners can clearly express their real challenges and operational talent can demonstrate capability around specific scenarios to find partners and co-create teams (前置筛选与需求匹配)
- **Curriculum categories:** **Content Operations (内容运营) · Activity Operations (活动运营) · Community Building (社区建设) · Contributor Development (贡献者发展)** — a four-category operational curriculum that this series' prior briefings have not documented as a formal open-source-foundation curriculum surface
- **Non-technical-participant inclusion:** "Non-technical-background participants can also find a concrete position to enter the AI open-source ecosystem through this path" (非技术背景的参与者也能由此找到进入AI开源生态的具体位置)
- **The Foundation's own diagnostic of the AI open-source ecosystem surface:** "Technology is only the starting point. How technical value is understood, how a project continues to attract participants, and how a single event can be deposited as long-term collaboration often determine whether it can move from a code repository to an active ecosystem" (技术只是起点，技术价值如何被理解，项目如何持续吸引参与者，一次活动如何沉淀为长期协作，往往决定了它能否从代码仓库走向活跃生态)
- **The Foundation's closing operational claim:** "Let talent grow in projects; let projects be active because of talent; and then let the Foundation deposit effective collaboration as a replicable and sustainable ecosystem mechanism" (让人才在项目中成长，让项目因人才而活跃，再由基金会把有效协作沉淀为可以复制可以持续的生态机制)

The four-category operational curriculum pairing — Content Operations · Activity Operations · Community Building · Contributor Development — is the first-documented Chinese-open-source-foundation four-category-operational-curriculum object in this series. The four categories sit institutionally alongside this series' September 18 briefing's documentation of the OpenAtom Foundation's three-module donor-training curriculum (Open Source Overview and Enterprise Value · Enterprise Internal Open Source and Open Source Governance Systems · Enterprise Participation and Open Source Project Creation) — the same Chinese-open-source-foundation training-and-certification surface this series has documented at the OpenAtom Foundation layer and the Mulan Foundation layer is being operationalized at the Tiangong Kaiwu Open Source Foundation operational-curriculum layer simultaneously — a three-foundation-simultaneous-Chinese-open-source-foundation-training-curriculum pattern.

The Tiangong Kaiwu Open Source Foundation's own diagnostic pairing — "technology is only the starting point; how technical value is understood; how a project continues to attract participants; how a single event can be deposited as long-term collaboration" — pairs institutionally with this series' September 16 briefing's documentation of the Huawei Open Source Official's first-systematic Chinese open-source cultural definition (开源无国界 开发者有来处) and its four-mechanism diagnostic (兴趣带来速度 · 使命选择方向 · 边界保证质量 · 制度支撑长久) — the same Chinese vendor-official cultural-definition surface this series' prior briefing documented at the Chinese-vendor-official-account cultural-definition layer is being operationalized at the Chinese open-source foundation self-declaration layer simultaneously. The Tiangong Kaiwu Open Source Foundation is making an institutionally parallel claim at the foundation layer that Huawei is making at the vendor-official layer: technical value alone is not sufficient to sustain an open-source ecosystem, and the operational layer (technology / community / culture / governance) is where the ecosystem's sustainability is decided.

The Foundation's closing operational claim — "let the Foundation deposit effective collaboration as a replicable and sustainable ecosystem mechanism" (再由基金会把有效协作沉淀为可以复制可以持续的生态机制) — pairs institutionally with this series' September 2 briefing's documentation of Chongqing's 15th-Five-Year-Plan AI framework's explicit "open-source infrastructure and compliance codification" clauses and this series' September 10 briefing's documentation of the Shanghai Open Source Industry Investment Fund as a municipal-scale capital-instrument object — the same Chinese-municipal-plus-foundation-layer open-source-ecosystem-institutionalization surface is being operationalized at the district-party-committee-talent-office co-hosting layer simultaneously with the municipal-plan layer and the municipal-capital-instrument layer.

The **Ecosystem Co-Builder Day (生态共建者日)** naming itself is the sharpest institutional-economics object of the day: this is the first-documented instance of a Chinese open-source foundation naming a formal open-source-ecosystem-day surface at the foundation's own layer. The Ecosystem Co-Builder Day naming pairs institutionally with this series' September 18 briefing's documentation of OpenAtom Foundation's donor-only-training layer (a Foundation-naming-a-donor-training-day-surface object) and this series' September 16 briefing's documentation of the Huawei Open Source Official's "code → project → ecosystem → business" four-crossing institutional-form model — the same Foundation-naming-an-ecosystem-day-surface object is being operationalized at the Tiangong Kaiwu Foundation layer, the OpenAtom Foundation donor-training layer, and the Huawei Open Source Official vendor-official four-crossing-institutional-form-model layer simultaneously — a three-surface-simultaneous-Foundation-and-vendor-official-ecosystem-naming pattern.

**Institutional significance: The Tiangong Kaiwu Open Source Foundation's AI Open-Source Ecosystem Operation Class and Ecosystem Co-Builder Day recruitment is the first-documented Chinese-municipal-district-party-committee-talent-office-plus-university-plus-open-source-foundation-plus-education-platform plus weekly-event co-hosting surface in this series, and it is being operationalized at four institutional surfaces simultaneously — the district-party-committee-talent-office-plus-foundation-plus-university-plus-education-platform co-hosting layer, the Content-Operations-plus-Activity-Operations-plus-Community-Building-plus-Contributor-Development four-category-operational-curriculum layer, the Foundation-self-declared "technology-is-only-the-starting-point" ecosystem-sustainability diagnostic layer, and the Ecosystem-Co-Builder-Day Foundation-naming-an-ecosystem-day-surface layer — a four-surface-simultaneous-Chinese-open-source-foundation-plus-municipal-district-party-committee-talent-office-institutionalization pattern.**

### Institutional Change — CAICT Launches the First Batch of Agent Management Ability Maturity Assessment (AOMA)

**1. CAICT WeChat (2026-09-19) — AI-Native · First Batch of Agent Management Ability Maturity Assessment (AOMA) Launched**

The China Academy of Information and Communications Technology (CAICT / 中国信息通信研究院), the Ministry-of-Industry-and-Information-Technology-affiliated research institute this series' prior briefings have documented at the AI-administrative-standard surface (September 14 briefing: MIIT "AI + Software" Special Action Implementation Plan), the AI-enterprise-support surface (September 6 briefing: MIIT AI SME Startup Support Plan 31号), and the CAICT-administrative-institute three-layer-governance-framework surface (September 17 briefing: CAICT Agent Governance Research Report 2026), launched on September 19, 2026 the first batch of **Agent Management Ability Maturity Assessment (智能体管理能力成熟度评估 / AOMA)** — the first-documented CAICT-administrative-institute three-tier-agent-assessment-instrument surface in this series.

The CAICT AOMA launch announcement's sharpest institutional-economics fact is that **CAICT has launched the assessment as part of a third-party assessment-and-testing system (智能原生系列评估测试体系) it is building** — the first-documented CAICT-administrative-institute self-declared third-party-assessment-and-testing-system object in this series. The CAICT is not positioning the AOMA as a technical-standards surface (like the SJ/T 12304-2026 industry-standard layer this series' September 19 briefing documented at the MIIT/CESI surface) or as a governance-framework surface (like the CAICT Agent Governance Research Report layer this series' September 17 briefing documented at the CAICT-administrative-institute-report surface) but as an **assessment-and-testing-instrument surface** — a structurally new institutional-form observation.

The CAICT AOMA launch announcement's operational profile, verbatim from the CAICT text:
- **Co-development consortium:** "CAICT together with dozens of enterprises (数十家企业) and nearly 100 experts (近百名专家)" jointly developed the assessment
- **Assessment launch:** The first batch of assessment work is now formally launched (首批评估工作正式启动)
- **The three core enterprise challenges the assessment targets:** (1) How to construct a systematic agent management system (如何构建系统化的智能体管理体系); (2) How to evaluate the level of an organization's agent-management capability (如何评估组织智能体管理能力水平); (3) How to promote agents from single-point applications to organizational-level capability (如何推动智能体从单点应用升级为组织级能力)
- **The diagnostic CAICT applies to the current state of Chinese enterprise agent deployment:** "Agent building presents a project-silo characteristic (项目孤岛化特征) with a lack of standardized processes and management requirements; full-lifecycle management disorder (全生命周期管理无序化) leads to uncontrollable quality, high delivery risk, and difficult replication and promotion" (缺乏标准化流程与管理要求，全生命周期管理无序化导致质量不可控，交付风险高，复制推广困难)
- **The two-axis management-domain architecture:** A two-axis (二维架构) management-domain architecture combining the **comprehensive management domain (统筹管理能力域)** and the **full-process management domain (全流程管理能力域)** — an architectural design intended to "help enterprises achieve the organic fusion of governance and execution" (帮助企业实现治理与执行的有机融合)
- **The five-level maturity framework:** The assessment divides agent-management ability into **five maturity levels — Entry (入门级) → Advanced (进阶级) → Excellent (优秀级) → Excellence (卓越级) → Leading (引领级)** — with each level setting explicit advancement requirements and quantified indicators (每级设置明确的进阶要求和量化指标)

The five-level AOMA maturity framework pairing — Entry → Advanced → Excellent → Excellence → Leading — is the first-documented Chinese-administrative-institute five-level-maturity-framework object in this series. From an institutional economics standpoint, this **five-level-maturity-framework / CAICT-administrative-institute layer pairing** is a **structural finding**: CAICT is operationalizing the CMMI-style maturity-model apparatus at the AI-agent-surface layer — a structurally parallel institutional-form move at the agent-surface layer to the CMMI-style software-engineering-maturity-model apparatus that the software-industry-institutional-history has documented historically at the software-engineering-surface layer. The two-axis management-domain architecture (comprehensive + full-process) is the CAICT's own architectural extension of the CMMI-style apparatus onto the agent-management surface.

The five-level AOMA maturity framework pairs institutionally with this series' September 19 briefing's documentation of the SJ/T 12304-2026 industry-standard two-axis open-source-grading framework (Completeness L 32 indicators + Openness O 35 indicators = 67 indicators) — the same Chinese-administrative-institute grading-and-maturity-framework apparatus is being operationalized at two institutionally distinct surfaces simultaneously: the SJ/T 12304-2026 industry-standard two-axis-67-indicator LLM-open-source-grading surface and the CAICT AOMA two-axis-five-level agent-management-maturity surface — a **two-surface-simultaneous-Chinese-administrative-institute grading-and-maturity-instrument pattern**.

The two-axis management-domain architecture pairing — Comprehensive Management Domain + Full-Process Management Domain — pairs institutionally with this series' September 17 briefing's documentation of the CAICT Policy-and-Economics Institute's three-layer agent-governance framework (Foundation layer data-and-identity base / Operation layer execution-and-dynamic-control / Ecosystem layer value-collaboration) — the same CAICT-administrative-institute agent-surface governance surface is being operationalized at the CAICT-administrative-institute three-layer-governance-framework layer and the CAICT-administrative-institute two-axis-maturity-architecture layer simultaneously — a two-layer-simultaneous-CAICT-administrative-institute-agent-governance-and-maturity-instrument pattern.

The CAICT AOMA launch announcement's sharpest institutional-economics diagnostic is CAICT's own claim that agent building currently "presents a project-silo characteristic (项目孤岛化特征) with a lack of standardized processes and management requirements" — the first-documented CAICT-administrative-institute diagnostic placing the current state of Chinese-enterprise agent deployment at the project-silo-and-standardization-lack layer. From an institutional economics standpoint, this **project-silo-and-standardization-lack diagnostic / CAICT-administrative-institute surface pairing** is a **structural finding**: CAICT is diagnosing the current Chinese-enterprise agent deployment surface as an **institutional-institutionalization-deficit** — the Chinese-enterprise agent surface is currently a project-silo surface with a lack of standardization, and CAICT is proposing a maturity-assessment-instrument solution to convert the project-silo surface into a standardized-organizational-capability surface. This is institutionally parallel to the "open-source low-spec" industry-convention this series' prior briefings have documented at multiple layers (September 17 briefing: Jiang Xiaojuan's State-Council-formal-level Bund Summit diagnostic of the "broken the industry's unspoken rule of open-source-as-low-spec" surface; September 15 briefing: GOSIM Shenzhen 2026's Chinese-vendor-official surfaces) — the same institutional-institutionalization-deficit diagnostic is being operationalized at the CAICT-administrative-institute layer and the State-Council-formal-level Bund Summit layer simultaneously.

The CAICT AOMA launch announcement pairs institutionally with this series' September 14 briefing's documentation of the MIIT "AI + Software" Special Action Implementation Plan's Agent Software Application Store (智能体软件应用商店) and Skills Resource Library (Skills 资源库) construction — the same MIIT-Central-Administrative-Plan and CAICT-Institute-Assessment-Instrument pairing on the same agent-distribution-and-agent-management institutional surface this series' prior briefings have been documenting at the MIIT-industrial-policy layer and the CAICT-administrative-institute-report layer. The MIIT is directing the construction of the state-directed Agent Software Application Store as a distribution-and-circulation institutional apparatus; the CAICT is launching the AOMA as a management-capability-assessment institutional apparatus. The two institutional surfaces operate in parallel on the same agent-surface institutional object — a two-surface-simultaneous-MIIT-Central-Administrative-Plan-and-CAICT-Institute-Assessment-Instrument pattern.

The CAICT AOMA's positioning as a **third-party assessment-and-testing system** is the sharpest institutional-economics object of the day: this is the first-documented CAICT-administrative-institute self-declared third-party-assessment-and-testing-system object in this series. From an institutional economics standpoint, this **CAICT-third-party-assessment-and-testing-system object / CAICT-administrative-institute surface pairing** is a **structural finding**: CAICT is positioning itself not as a regulator (which would be the CAC's institutional role this series' September 3 briefing documented at the CAC Qinglang Phase 2 layer), not as a standard-setter (which would be the CESI's institutional role this series' September 19 briefing documented at the SJ/T 12304-2026 industry-standard layer), but as a **third-party assessor** of enterprise agent-management capability — a structurally new institutional-form move at the CAICT-institute-assessment-instrument layer. The CAICT is placing itself at an assessment-and-testing institutional surface distinct from both the CAC-regulatory-control surface and the CESI-industry-standard-setting surface this series' prior briefings have documented.

**Institutional significance: CAICT's Agent Management Ability Maturity Assessment (AOMA) launch is the first-documented CAICT-administrative-institute three-tier-agent-assessment-instrument surface in this series, and it is being operationalized at five institutional surfaces simultaneously — the CAICT-third-party-assessment-and-testing-system layer, the two-axis-management-domain-architecture layer (Comprehensive + Full-Process), the five-level-maturity-framework layer (Entry → Advanced → Excellent → Excellence → Leading), the CAICT-plus-50-enterprise-plus-100-expert co-development-consortium layer, and the project-silo-and-standardization-lack diagnostic layer — a five-surface-simultaneous-CAICT-administrative-institute-agent-assessment-instrument pattern.**

### Commentary

**Two institutionally new placements on the same unsolved institutional object that this series' prior thirty-one briefings have been documenting: the institutional identity of Chinese open source across institutional borders.**

This cycle's briefing documents a structurally new institutional pattern: the same Chinese open-source surface is being operationalized simultaneously at two institutionally distinct surfaces this series' prior briefings have not documented at before — the Chinese-municipal-district-party-committee-talent-office-plus-open-source-foundation-plus-university-plus-education-platform-plus-weekly-event co-hosting layer (Tiangong Kaiwu Open Source Foundation co-hosting the AI Open-Source Ecosystem Operation Class with the Xihu District Committee Talent Office, Hangzhou Dianzi University, and Datawhale, launching the inaugural Ecosystem Co-Builder Day during the GOAI Open Source Week, with a four-category operational curriculum of Content Operations · Activity Operations · Community Building · Contributor Development and a self-declared "technology is only the starting point" ecosystem-sustainability diagnostic), and the CAICT-administrative-institute three-tier-agent-assessment-instrument layer (CAICT launching the first batch of Agent Management Ability Maturity Assessment, positioning itself as a third-party assessment-and-testing system, building a two-axis management-domain architecture of Comprehensive Management Domain + Full-Process Management Domain with a five-level maturity framework of Entry → Advanced → Excellent → Excellence → Leading, jointly with 50+ enterprises and nearly 100 experts, targeting the diagnostic of project-silo-and-standardization-lack as the current state of Chinese-enterprise agent deployment).

The two placements are institutionally coherent on the same unsolved institutional object — the institutional identity of Chinese open source across institutional borders — and the two placements together document a **two-surface-simultaneous-institutionalization pattern** alongside the three-surface-simultaneous pattern this series' September 19 briefing documented at the Chinese-administrative industry-standard layer (SJ/T 12304-2026) and the Reuters-English-language-international-wire institutional layer (DeepSeek's hiring of Yan Wentao as its first CFO ahead of a possible STAR Market IPO).

**One structural pattern across the two placements: each placement pairs institutionally with prior briefings' documented surfaces, and the two placements together document that the same Chinese open-source surface is being operationalized at two institutionally distinct surfaces simultaneously — an institutionally deeper two-surface-simultaneous pattern than the two-surface-simultaneous pattern this series' September 19 briefing documented, because the two surfaces this cycle's briefing documents sit at institutional layers this series' prior briefings have not documented at before.**

- The Tiangong Kaiwu Open Source Foundation + Xihu District Committee Talent Office placement pairs institutionally with this series' August 8 briefing's Tiangong Kaiwu-plus-CAICT co-founding of the Open Agentic AI Foundation (OAAIF) layer, this series' September 2 briefing's Chongqing 15th-Five-Year-Plan AI framework's open-source-infrastructure-and-compliance codification layer, this series' September 10 briefing's Shanghai Open Source Industry Investment Fund municipal-scale capital-instrument layer, this series' September 16 briefing's Huawei Open Source Official first-systematic Chinese open-source cultural-definition layer (开源无国界 开发者有来处), and this series' September 18 briefing's OpenAtom Foundation donor-only open-source general-knowledge training program layer — the same Chinese open-source foundation surface being operationalized at the Chinese-municipal-district-party-committee-talent-office-plus-foundation-plus-university-plus-education-platform co-hosting layer simultaneously with the CAICT-co-founded-OAAIF layer, the municipal-plan layer, the municipal-capital-instrument layer, the Chinese-vendor-official cultural-definition layer, and the OpenAtom-Founder-donor-training layer.
- The CAICT AOMA placement pairs institutionally with this series' September 17 briefing's CAICT Policy-and-Economics Institute three-layer agent-governance framework at the CAICT-administrative-institute-report layer, this series' September 14 briefing's MIIT "AI + Software" Special Action Implementation Plan's Agent Software Application Store + Skills Resource Library at the MIIT-industrial-policy layer, and this series' September 19 briefing's SJ/T 12304-2026 industry-standard two-axis open-source-grading framework at the MIIT/CESI layer — the same agent-surface and Chinese-administrative-institute grading-and-maturity-instrument surface being operationalized at the CAICT-administrative-institute third-party-assessment-and-testing-system layer simultaneously with the CAICT-administrative-institute-report layer, the MIIT-industrial-policy layer, and the MIIT/CESI-industry-standard layer.

**One structural risk across the two placements: the same Chinese open-source surface is being operationalized at two institutionally distinct surfaces simultaneously, and the same "open-source" surface is being absorbed into multiple institutional mechanisms simultaneously at two institutionally distinct layers — a two-surface-simultaneous-institutional-absorption pattern.**

The Tiangong Kaiwu Open Source Foundation + Xihu District Committee Talent Office placement is being operationalized at the Chinese-municipal-district-party-committee-talent-office co-hosting layer, and if the Chinese-municipal-district-party-committee-talent-office layer absorbs the AI open-source ecosystem operation-class surface into a district-party-committee-talent-office-talent-pipeline object rather than an open-community-operation-class object, the AI open-source ecosystem operation-class surface converts into a district-party-committee-talent-office-talent-pipeline object.

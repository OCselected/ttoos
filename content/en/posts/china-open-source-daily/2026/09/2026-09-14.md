⚠️ **Editorial note: The open source ecosystem in China operates under a distinct institutional framework — characterized by state-led initiatives, intranet-like boundaries, and top-down governance. Readers should be aware that this context differs from the community-driven open source model common in other regions. The term "open source" as used in Chinese media may refer to practices that diverge from the conventional definition.**

## China Open Source Daily — 2026-09-14

### 🏛️ Institutional Design Anchor — MIIT's "AI + Software" Special Action Implementation Plan (人工智能+软件专项行动实施方案): The Seventh Surface of State-Directed Open-Source Institutional Formalization

**1. Sina Tech / IT之家 (September 11, 2026) — "工信部印发'AI + 软件'专项行动实施方案：支持软件企业与智能手机、智能家居、智能网联汽车等终端厂商联合创新"**

**2. Sina Finance (September 11, 2026) — "工信部印发'人工智能+软件'专项行动实施方案，到2028年覆盖2万家规模以上软件企业"**

**3. Tencent News (September 11, 2026) — "工信部部署'AI+软件'，五年后这些新业态要成为产业新增长极"**

**4. EET China (September 11, 2026) — "工信部印发《'人工智能+软件'方案》：2028年覆盖2万家规上软件企业"**

**5. IT Home (September 11, 2026) — "我国力争到 2030 年关键软件全面实现智能化升级"**

MIIT issued on September 11, 2026 — the same week as this series' September 11 briefing documented the Chen Kaihua Qiushi party-theoretical-flagship placement and the Qinghe Zhishashe independent-institutional-economics diagnostic — a national industrial-policy document: **the "AI + Software" Special Action Implementation Plan (人工智能+软件专项行动实施方案)**. The document is a state-directed industrial-policy instrument on the software-and-information-services industrial surface, and its open-source clauses — the only clauses this briefing analyzes — make five institutionally new moves on the same unsolved object that this series' prior twenty-four briefings have been tracking.

The two documented numerical targets of the plan, verbatim from the Sina Tech / IT之家 report:

> "By 2028, the software and information-technology-services industry's intelligence-transformation will see significant improvement... incubate 5 or more quality open-source projects (5个以上优质开源项目)."

> "By 2030, achieve a new leap in the fusion-development of AI with the software and information-technology-services industry... build multiple open-source communities and industrial clusters with international influence (建成若干具有国际影响力的开源社区和产业集群)."

This is the first documented instance of a national industrial-policy document attaching quantitative open-source-project targets (5+ quality open-source projects by 2028, multiple internationally influential open-source communities by 2030) to the industrial-policy horizon. The September 6 briefing's AI SME Startup Support Plan (31号) documented open-source-community designation and open-source-contributor recognition; the September 11 plan documented the same open-source surface at the **state-directed-open-source-project-selection / quantitative-open-source-project-target / international-open-source-community-target** layer — the first time a Chinese national industrial-policy document attaches quantitative open-source targets to the industrial-policy horizon.

The five institutionally new moves documented in the plan's open-source clauses, extracted verbatim from the Sina Tech / IT之家 report and cross-referenced with the EET China and Tencent News reports:

**Move 1 — The Agent Software Application Store (智能体软件应用商店) and Skills Resource Library (Skills 资源库): the first state-directed institutional apparatus for agent-software distribution.**

The plan reads: "推动建设智能体软件应用商店和技能包（Skills）资源库，规范平台上架审核与运营管理，促进智能体、技能包广泛应用" (Advance the construction of Agent Software Application Stores and Skill-Pack (Skills) Resource Libraries, standardize platform listing review and operational management, and promote the widespread application of agents and skill-packs).

The same paragraph reads: "培育智能体开源专区，构建多元协同、技能共享的开放生态" (Cultivate an agent open-source zone, constructing a plural-collaboration, skill-sharing open ecosystem).

This is institutionally the sharpest move in the plan's open-source clauses: **MIIT is directing the construction of a state-directed Agent Software Application Store and Skills Resource Library as institutional apparatus for agent-software distribution and skill-pack distribution**. The same distribution-surface move this series' September 3 briefing documented at the Android-app-store layer (the CAC Qinglang Phase 2 announcement naming Huawei, Xiaomi, OPPO, and vivo app stores as AI-app-admission subjects) is now being operationalized at the agent-software layer — a structurally parallel institutional-form move at a different distribution surface. From an institutional economics standpoint, this **state-directed-agent-app-store / android-app-store-admission pairing** is a **structural finding**: the state-directed-app-store institutional-form is being operationalized at two distribution surfaces simultaneously — the traditional Android-app-distribution surface (September 3 CAC briefing) and the agent-software-distribution surface (September 11 plan) — with the two surfaces operating in parallel on the same institutional-form object.

**Move 2 — Independent open-source license application and compliance guidance (自主开源许可协议应用与合规指引): the first administrative-document use of Mulan-family-license promotion as a national industrial-policy instrument.**

The plan reads: "依托全国性开源基金会与人工智能开源社区，引导企业开放智能编程、软件产品智能化、智能体等成果，支持重点开源项目在社区首发，设立智能操作系统等领域开源专区，加强自主开源许可协议应用与合规指引" (Relying on national open-source foundations and AI open-source communities, guide enterprises to open-source their results in smart programming, software-product-intelligence, and agents, support key open-source projects in community-first release, establish open-source zones in fields such as intelligent operating systems, and strengthen independent open-source license application and compliance guidance).

The clause "加强自主开源许可协议应用与合规指引" (strengthen independent open-source license application and compliance guidance) is institutionally the sharpest legal-and-licensing move in the plan: **it is the first administrative-document use of Mulan-family-license promotion as a national industrial-policy instrument**. From an institutional economics standpoint, this **independent-license-compliance / national-industrial-policy pairing** is a **structural finding**: the Mulan-family-license infrastructure (MulanPSL, MulanPSL-2.0, MulanPOM, Mulan-DSL — documented in this series' August 30 briefing on the Mulan Foundation and in the September 9 briefing on the openKylin × Astron-Agent challenge's MulanPSL-2.0 licensing) is now being formally placed inside the national industrial-policy document as a compliance-guidance instrument — the Mulan-family-license apparatus is no longer a community-level or foundation-level licensing instrument but is a **state-directed national-industrial-policy instrument**.

**Move 3 — Quality open-source project selection-and-cultivation mechanism with community-first-release (社区首发): the first administrative-document institutionalization of a state-directed open-source-project selection-and-cultivation apparatus paired with a community-first-release operationalization.**

The plan reads: "建立优质开源项目遴选和培育机制，鼓励探索多元化商业模式，推动优质项目在制造、金融等领域深度应用" (Establish a quality-open-source-project selection-and-cultivation mechanism, encourage exploration of diverse business models, and promote deep application of quality projects in manufacturing, finance, and other fields).

The clause "支持重点开源项目在社区首发" (support key open-source projects in community-first release) — embedded in Move 2's paragraph — is institutionally the sharpest structural finding of this cycle on the community-first-release surface: **the plan pairs a state-directed project-selection-and-cultivation mechanism with a community-first-release operationalization** — the state selects quality open-source projects and requires them to be first-released at a community rather than at an enterprise. From an institutional economics standpoint, this **state-directed-project-selection / community-first-release pairing** is a **structural finding**: the plan is not just creating a state-directed open-source-project selection mechanism but is pairing it with a community-first-release operationalization — the same "community-first-release" operationalization this series' August 19 briefing documented in the context of the AtomGit national-level open-source-community designation, now being operationalized at the state-directed-project-selection surface.

**Move 4 — Open-source contributions incorporated into research-evaluation and talent-certification systems (科研评价与人才认定): the sharpest administrative absorption of open-source community norms at the higher-education and research-institute layer.**

The plan reads: "完善开源贡献激励，支持高校、科研院所和企业将开源贡献纳入科研评价与人才认定体系，探索将开源贡献作为挖掘软件领域优质中小企业重要参考指标" (Improve open-source contribution incentives, support universities, research institutes, and enterprises in incorporating open-source contributions into research-evaluation and talent-certification systems, and explore using open-source contributions as an important reference indicator for identifying quality SMEs in the software field).

This is institutionally the sharpest move in the plan's open-source clauses: **it extends the September 6 briefing's star-count-as-administrative-metric move (which placed GitHub-style star counts alongside invention patents and highly-cited papers as one of four national-level evaluation indicators) into a broader research-evaluation-incorporation move at the higher-education and research-institute layer**. The September 6 briefing documented the star-count-as-administrative-metric move as the first administrative-document use of the GitHub-style star metric as an official institutional evaluation indicator, positioned alongside invention patents and highly-cited papers (both administrative-metric indicators). The September 11 plan extends that move into a broader **research-evaluation-incorporation** move — universities, research institutes, and enterprises are all directed to incorporate open-source contributions into their internal research-evaluation and talent-certification systems, and open-source contributions are additionally being explored as a reference indicator for identifying quality SMEs in the software field. From an institutional economics standpoint, this **star-count-administrative-metric / research-evaluation-incorporation pairing** is a **structural finding**: the administrative absorption of open-source community norms has moved from the star-count-as-administrative-metric layer (September 6 briefing) to the research-evaluation-incorporation layer (September 11 plan) — the same open-source-contribution object is being absorbed at two administrative-metric layers simultaneously.

**Move 5 — Quantitative open-source-project targets (5+ quality open-source projects by 2028, multiple internationally influential open-source communities by 2030): the first national industrial-policy document attaching quantitative open-source targets to the industrial-policy horizon.**

The plan's 2028 target reads: "开放协同的开源社区建设取得积极进展，孵化 5 个以上优质开源项目" (open-collaborative open-source community construction achieves positive progress, incubating 5 or more quality open-source projects). The plan's 2030 target reads: "建成若干具有国际影响力的开源社区和产业集群" (build multiple open-source communities and industrial clusters with international influence).

The pairing of these two targets is institutionally the sharpest structural finding of this cycle on the quantitative-open-source-target surface: **the plan is the first national industrial-policy document attaching quantitative open-source-project targets (5+ quality open-source projects by 2028) and qualitative open-source-community targets (multiple internationally influential open-source communities by 2030) to the industrial-policy horizon**. The 2028 target (5+ quality open-source projects) is being paired with the plan's Move 3 (state-directed open-source-project selection-and-cultivation mechanism with community-first-release) — the quantitative target is being operationalized through the state-directed project-selection-and-cultivation apparatus. The 2030 target (multiple internationally influential open-source communities) is being paired with this series' prior briefings' documentation of the Western-foundation-governance-seat surface (PyTorch Foundation, September 10 briefing; AAIF, September 7 briefing) and the party-theoretical-flagship surface (Chen Kaihua Qiushi, September 11 briefing) — the internationally-influential-open-source-community target is being operationalized through the same Western-foundation-governance-seat and party-theoretical-flagship surfaces that this series' prior briefings have been documenting.

**Institutional significance: The AI + Software Special Action Implementation Plan is a structurally new placement on the same unsolved object that this series' prior twenty-four briefings have been documenting — the institutional identity of Chinese open source across institutional borders — and it is the sharpest documented formalization of open-source as a state-directed industrial-policy instrument at the national-industrial-policy surface.**

From an institutional economics perspective, the AI + Software Special Action Implementation Plan matters on five axes, and each of the five axes pairs institutionally with a prior briefing's documented surface:

**First, the state-directed Agent Software Application Store + Skills Resource Library pairing pairs institutionally with this series' September 3 briefing's Android-app-store-admission documentation (CAC Qinglang Phase 2, naming Huawei, Xiaomi, OPPO, vivo app stores as AI-app-admission subjects).** The state-directed-app-store institutional-form is being operationalized at two distribution surfaces simultaneously — the traditional Android-app-distribution surface and the agent-software-distribution surface — with the same institutional-form object (state-directed app-store) at the two surfaces.

**Second, the independent open-source license application and compliance guidance (自主开源许可协议应用与合规指引) move pairs institutionally with this series' August 30 briefing's Mulan Foundation documentation and September 9 briefing's openKylin × Astron-Agent challenge MulanPSL-2.0 licensing.** The Mulan-family-license apparatus is no longer a community-level or foundation-level licensing instrument but is a state-directed national-industrial-policy instrument.

**Third, the state-directed open-source-project selection-and-cultivation mechanism with community-first-release (社区首发) pairing pairs institutionally with this series' August 19 briefing's AtomGit national-level open-source-community designation documentation.** The community-first-release operationalization is now being paired with a state-directed project-selection-and-cultivation mechanism.

**Fourth, the research-evaluation-incorporation move (open-source contributions into research-evaluation and talent-certification systems) extends the September 6 briefing's star-count-as-administrative-metric move (GitHub-style star counts as one of four national-level evaluation indicators alongside invention patents and highly-cited papers).** The administrative absorption of open-source community norms has moved from the star-count-as-administrative-metric layer to the research-evaluation-incorporation layer.

**Fifth, the quantitative open-source-project targets (5+ quality open-source projects by 2028, multiple internationally influential open-source communities by 2030) pair institutionally with this series' September 10 briefing's PyTorch Foundation Western-foundation-governance-seat documentation (Alibaba Cloud and Cambricon as Platinum members with Governing Board + Technical Advisory Council seats, Ant Group as Gold member) and September 11 briefing's Chen Kaihua Qiushi party-theoretical-flagship documentation (proposal for a national-level AI-research open-source community).** The internationally-influential-open-source-community target is being operationalized through the same Western-foundation-governance-seat and party-theoretical-flagship surfaces.

### 🔍 Commentary

**One institutional-design anchor (MIIT's "AI + Software" Special Action Implementation Plan) on the same unsolved institutional object that this series' prior twenty-four briefings have been documenting: the institutional identity of Chinese open source across institutional borders.**

This cycle's briefing documents a structurally new placement on the same unsolved institutional object that the prior twenty-four briefings have been tracking — **the institutional identity of Chinese open source across institutional borders** — and the placement is a specific state-directed-industrial-policy-instrument move at the national-industrial-policy surface.

The AI + Software Special Action Implementation Plan makes five institutionally new moves on the same unsolved institutional object as this series' prior briefings — the state-directed Agent Software Application Store + Skills Resource Library pairing (a distribution-surface move), the independent open-source license application and compliance guidance move (a licensing-and-legal move), the state-directed open-source-project selection-and-cultivation mechanism with community-first-release pairing (a project-selection-and-cultivation move), the research-evaluation-incorporation move (a research-evaluation-and-talent-certification move), and the quantitative open-source-project targets pairing (a quantitative-target move).

**One structural pattern across the five moves: each move pairs institutionally with a prior briefing's documented surface, and the five moves together document that the state-directed-industrial-policy surface has crossed into the seventh surface of state-directed open-source institutional formalization.**

- The state-directed Agent Software Application Store + Skills Resource Library pairing pairs institutionally with the September 3 briefing's Android-app-store-admission documentation (CAC Qinglang Phase 2) — the same state-directed-app-store institutional-form operationalized at two distribution surfaces.
- The independent open-source license application and compliance guidance move pairs institutionally with the August 30 briefing's Mulan Foundation documentation and September 9 briefing's openKylin × Astron-Agent challenge MulanPSL-2.0 licensing — the Mulan-family-license apparatus placed inside the national industrial-policy document as a compliance-guidance instrument.
- The state-directed open-source-project selection-and-cultivation mechanism with community-first-release pairing pairs institutionally with the August 19 briefing's AtomGit national-level open-source-community designation documentation.
- The research-evaluation-incorporation move extends the September 6 briefing's star-count-as-administrative-metric move at the national-industrial-policy layer.
- The quantitative open-source-project targets pairing pairs institutionally with the September 10 briefing's PyTorch Foundation Western-foundation-governance-seat documentation and September 11 briefing's Chen Kaihua Qiushi party-theoretical-flagship documentation.

**One structural risk across the five moves: the same state-directed-industrial-policy apparatus is being operationalized at multiple institutional surfaces simultaneously, and the same "community" surface is being absorbed into multiple state-directed mechanisms simultaneously.**

The state-directed Agent Software Application Store, Skills Resource Library, and Agent Open-Source Zone are being operationalized at the agent-software-distribution surface; the state-directed open-source-project selection-and-cultivation mechanism with community-first-release is being operationalized at the project-selection-and-cultivation surface; the independent open-source license compliance guidance is being operationalized at the licensing-and-legal surface; the research-evaluation-incorporation is being operationalized at the higher-education and research-institute surface. The same "community" surface — the operational community where open-source contribution actually happens — is being absorbed into multiple state-directed mechanisms simultaneously (the app-store, the selection-and-cultivation, the license-compliance, the research-evaluation-incorporation, the quantitative-target surfaces).

The institutional-economics question — which this cycle's briefing documents but does not answer — is **whether the Chinese institutional architecture can hold the seven institutional surfaces simultaneously without one converting the other**. If the state-directed Agent Software Application Store absorbs the traditional Android-app-distribution surface (CAC Qinglang Phase 2) into a state-directed-agent-software-distribution surface, the state-directed-app-store institutional-form converts into a state-directed-distribution-monopoly object rather than a state-directed-distribution-instrument object; if the independent open-source license compliance guidance (Mulan-family) converts the Mulan Foundation from a community-level licensing foundation into a national-industrial-policy compliance-guidance apparatus, the Mulan Foundation's community-level institutional-form converts into a state-directed-licensing-instrument object; if the research-evaluation-incorporation move converts universities and research institutes' research-evaluation systems into open-source-contribution-capture systems rather than open-source-contribution-recognition systems, the research-evaluation-incorporation move becomes an open-source-contribution-capture apparatus rather than an open-source-contribution-incentive apparatus.

**One perspective, not a verdict.**

The AI + Software Special Action Implementation Plan is best read as **an observation of institutional movement in progress**, not as a verdict on institutional direction. The plan does not guarantee that the state-directed Agent Software Application Store will be operationalized as a state-directed-distribution-instrument rather than a state-directed-distribution-monopoly; the plan does not guarantee that the independent open-source license compliance guidance will maintain Mulan's community-level institutional-form rather than convert into a state-directed-licensing-instrument apparatus; the plan does not guarantee that the research-evaluation-incorporation move will remain an open-source-contribution-incentive apparatus rather than convert into an open-source-contribution-capture apparatus. What this cycle's briefing documents is that the state-directed-industrial-policy surface has crossed into the seventh surface of state-directed open-source institutional formalization — from a six-surface-simultaneous-formalization pattern (September 6 briefing's documentation of the AI SME Startup Support Plan 31号 as the sixth surface) to a **seven-surface-simultaneous-formalization pattern** — and that the seventh surface is the one that pairs state-directed-app-store / independent-license-compliance / community-first-release / research-evaluation-incorporation / quantitative-project-target moves into a single national-industrial-policy document, with each of the five moves pairing institutionally with a prior briefing's documented surface.
